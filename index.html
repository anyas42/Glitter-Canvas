AI Glitter Canvas

Welcome to an interactive digital art installation. Draw magical sparkling glitter in the air using your laptop webcam and natural hand gestures.

☝️ Index Up: Draw Raise index fingertip to paint glittering trails.
🖐️ Open Palm: Erase Spread your hand to wipe away glitter smoothly.
✊ Fist: Pause Curl fingers to hover pointer without drawing.
✌️ Two Fingers: Color Hold peace sign to cycle between 8 glitter palettes.
👌 Pinch: Sparkle Burst Pinch thumb & index to explode a shower of sparkles.

Webcam video stays 100% private in your browser. Also supports mouse/trackpad drawing!

Hand Gesture Guide

Position your hand comfortably within the webcam frame. Use these 5 intuitive gestures:

☝️ Index Finger: Draw Keep index extended and other fingers curled. Continuous glitter sparkles follow your fingertip.
🖐️ Open Palm: Erase Extend all fingers wide. A soft holographic eraser circle appears around your hand to wipe away strokes.
✊ Closed Fist: Pause Clench your fist to freely move your hand across the screen without painting or erasing.
✌️ Two Fingers: Change Color Hold index & middle fingers up. Watch the blue charging ring fill up to cycle to the next radiant color.
👌 Pinch: Sparkle Burst Bring thumb tip and index tip together to detonate a spectacular firework explosion of floating star sparkles.
